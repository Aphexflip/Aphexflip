# Engineering and Projects

Erik Erling is an industrial equipment service and maintenance engineer who also develops independent software and AI projects through RSYMO.

His work combines industrial troubleshooting, automation, software development, and an interest in making complex systems easier to use.

Current projects include:

- **RSYMO and Taskmaster:** A private dashboard and AI task orchestration system that is still being developed and tested.
- **Autonomous world simulation:** An experiment with persistent AI characters, goals, memory, and changing events.
- **[RSYMO Control sample](samples/rsymo-control-core/README.md):** A small public code example from the orchestration work, with automated tests.
- **[Suno Surgery](https://github.com/Aphexflip/suno-surgery):** Experimental local tools for editing AI generated music.

The larger systems are private. The public links show specific examples, not complete finished products.
